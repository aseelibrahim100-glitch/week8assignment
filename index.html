My Three Favorite Food Trucks

Food trucks are one of my favorite ways to try new food without having to go to a restaurant. Below I'll write about my three favorite food trucks, where you can find them, what they serve, and why I love them.

Food Truck Details
Food Truck Name Truck Location Truck Photo Food Type Why It's My Favorite
Halal Original Food Cart 7258 Bustleton Ave #1224, Philadelphia, PA 19149 Middle Eastern and Mediterranean cuisine It's my favorite because it's the best halal truck I've had. The chicken is so flavorful, they give big portions, and the red sauce is so good.
La Marqueza 1700 Spring Garden St, Philadelphia, PA 19130 Mexican street food I like this truck because the burritos are delicious and the service is quick.
Frituras Sabor de Campo 100 W Ashdale St, Philadelphia, PA 19120 Latin American street food I like this truck because there is so much to choose from and the prices are reasonable.
